## FAQs

**Is a 25L geyser enough for a family of 4?**
For one bathroom, generally yes. A 25L tank is the size most Indian families choose for bathing. Larger families or homes with more bathrooms may need a second geyser.

**Is the Bajaj Shield Series New Shakti suitable for high-rise apartments?**
Yes. It is rated for up to 8 bar pressure, which suits most high-rise buildings with pressure pumps.

**What is the warranty on the Bajaj New Shakti 25L?**
The listing states 10 years on the tank. Bajaj also advertises 6 years on the heating element and 4 years on the product for this series, so confirm the exact terms on the Amazon listing.

**Does it include installation?**
A multi-function valve comes in the box. Installation terms vary, and pipes or extra fittings may be charged separately, so check with the seller or Bajaj service before ordering.

**How much electricity does a 2000W geyser use?**
While heating, it draws 2000W, or about 2 units per hour. The thermostat switches it off once the water reaches the set temperature, and the PUF insulation helps the water stay hot so it doesn't need to reheat as often.
