## FAQs

**Is an oil-filled heater better than a fan heater?**
For long use in a bedroom, usually yes. OFRs heat evenly and quietly, don't have a glowing element, and dry out the air less. Fan heaters are cheaper and heat faster, which makes them better for short bursts in small rooms.

**How much electricity does the Havells Hestio 13 Fin OFR use?**
At full power with the fan on, it draws up to 2900W, close to 3 units per hour. On lower settings it uses less, and the thermostat switches the heating on and off once the room is warm, which reduces the total.

**Do I need a special socket for this heater?**
Yes. It needs a 16A socket. Avoid extension boards and multi-plug adaptors.

**Is it safe to leave on overnight?**
It has a tip-over tilt switch, overheat protection and ISI certification, and OFRs are generally preferred for overnight use. Still, keep it away from curtains, bedding and clothes, and follow the manual.
